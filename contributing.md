**Why smoothing is the whole game**

An instant snap to the head is not a movement a hand produces. Smoothing spreads
the travel over several frames so the crosshair moves like a mouse. It is the
single setting that decides whether anyone notices.

**Tuning order**

1. **Hitbox** first — chest is the safe default.
2. **FOV** small. Raise it only if you are actually missing.
3. **Smoothing** last — this is what the aim looks like to other players.

**A mistake people make**

Cranking FOV to 180 and leaving smoothing at 5. That is the most visible
configuration possible. If you change one value for safety, lower the FOV.
